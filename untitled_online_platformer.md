## Group Project Information

**Project Name:** 
** untitled online platformer **

**Project Description:** 
** untitled online platformer ** is a web-based, 2D platformer game inspired by *Super Mario Maker*. 
The platform allows players to create, test, and share custom platforming levels using an intuitive, grid-based drag-and-drop editor. 
Creators can place terrain, hazards, enemies, and power-ups, adjust camera behavior (e.g., auto-scroll options), and tag their creations with difficulty levels ranging from Easy to Super Hard. 
The system supports online level sharing through a centralized database, permitting players to browse community levels or enter direct codes to access friends' stages. 
Additionally, the platform supports peer-to-peer online co-op multiplayer lobbies, local level downloads for offline gameplay, and user account management to track created levels, completion times, and community ratings.
