# Team 30

Team 30  is creating an application similar in purpose to Letterboxd, but for music instead of movies. Our app will be primarily used to log songs that users have listened to and allow them to rate and review those songs. There will also be a song recommendation system that will recommend songs to users based on their recent listening history. Spotify's API will be integrated to create a database of songs, artists, and genres that will make recommendation easier. Also, Spotify's API will allow uesrs to automatically log and track their listening history if they opt to do so.
