# Evently
---

## Description 

Evently is a web app that helps people in the Knoxville area discover local events and activities happening in their community. 
The platform aggregates event information from multiple sources, making it easier for users to find everything happening in one place. 
Users can browse or search for events based on their interests and narrow the results using filters such as location, date, price, and categories. 
Each event provides important information, including when and where the event takes place, and any other relevant information. 
Businesses and other organizations can also share information about their events, helping them reach a larger local audience.
