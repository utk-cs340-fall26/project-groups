# TargetTrack

## Project Description

TargetTrack is a web-based recruiting and application management platform that helps students monitor target companies for relevant internship and entry-level opportunities and manage those opportunities through the application process.

The central purpose of TargetTrack is to shift the recruiting workflow from manually searching for opportunities to proactively receiving relevant opportunities. Users establish a personalized target list by selecting companies and defining their job preferences. TargetTrack monitors supported career sources, identifies newly available positions that match those preferences, and notifies the user. Users can then save relevant positions and manage their progress through an integrated application tracker.
