## Project Name: Ufind

## Product Overview:
    The lost & found board is a web-based platform allowing students and admins to report lost items, report found items, and filter/search through active posts to locate a match. It replaces the often full or inconvenient office locations for a centralized, always-available online digital board that allows users to check or contribute to easily.
