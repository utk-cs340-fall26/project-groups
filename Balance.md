# Balance

# Project Description
This platform will be used to to integrate academic responsibilities, personal well being, and social communication all into one spot. In this application, a user will be able to focus on their health and wellbeing both alone and alongside others. Balance allows students to manage stress as well as improve their mental health by connecting with other college students to go on walks, organize workout classes or going to the gym together. This platform would ensure daily check-ins, a social way to get active, and a new way to keep track of assignments well in advance.
