# Project Name: Vol Dining Social
## Project Description
Vol Dining Social is an interactive dining forum for students at the University of Tennessee. The platform allows students to view dish ratings and comments based on the daily dining hall menus. Vol Dining Social provides a personalized experience based on previous ratings, dietary requirements, and community opinions.
